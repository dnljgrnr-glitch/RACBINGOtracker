Finding or Adding a Customer

  1. Go to Play Round.
  2. Type the customer's name in the search box.
  3. Click their name if they already exist, or click "+ Add ... as new customer" if they don't.

Setting Up Their Card

  1. Once a customer is selected, click Generate Random Card for an instant valid card, or type in all 24 numbers by hand from a physical card.
  2. Click Print Card (Front/Back) to hand them a physical copy — print the front, flip the card over, then print the back.

Starting and Playing a Round

  1. Once the card shows "Card ready ✓", click Start Round.
  2. As each ball is called from the machine, type the number and click Draw (or press Enter). There's no limit — keep drawing for as long as the customer wants to play, all the way to a full-card blackout if they get there.
  3. If the same number gets pulled twice, the app rejects it and tells the customer to pull another ball — it's never a dead end, they just keep drawing until it's a new number.
  4. Made a mistake? Click any drawn ball to remove just that one, or click Undo Last Draw for the most recent one.
  5. Watch the Live Card Progress grid — matched numbers highlight green as you go, and the full draw history stays visible the entire time.

Wins and Celebrations

  1. The app checks for a win after every ball — Line = $25, Four Corners = $50, Full BINGO = $100.
  2. Each tier only pays out once per round. Once any single row, column, or diagonal completes, that's the one $25 Line prize — finishing more lines afterward doesn't add another. The next milestone up is Four Corners, then Full BINGO.
  3. A win triggers fireworks and a popup, then Continue Playing ▶ keeps the round going with all progress intact, so they can keep drawing toward the next milestone. A round is worth one certificate, not one per milestone — it always reflects their current running total, up to $175 ($25 + $50 + $100) if they reach a full Blackout.

Submitting or Canceling a Round

  1. Click Submit Round / Next Customer once the customer is completely done playing — this saves every ball drawn and any wins, and frees up the machine for the next person.
  2. Started a round by mistake — wrong customer, or just need to scrap it? Click Cancel Round instead — nothing gets saved, and their weekly play status is untouched.

Redeeming a Reward

  1. Go to History. Every round with a win shows its total prize as one line — e.g. "Row 3, Four Corners — $75 total" — not a separate line per milestone.
  2. Click Confirm Redemption & New Card to log today's date against the round's full total and jump straight to starting their next round. A customer can redeem at any point — you don't need to wait for them to reach Blackout.
  3. Click Print Certificate anytime, before or after redeeming, to hand over (or reprint) their certificate — it always shows the round's current total, and reaching Full BINGO turns it into a grand $175 certificate automatically.

Fixing Mistakes (Any Time)

  1. In History, click Edit Round on any past round to reassign it to the correct customer, add or remove drawn balls, or delete the round entirely.
  2. Editing balls automatically rechecks whether the round should show a win, so prizes stay accurate.
  3. Deleting a round cannot be undone — double-check before confirming.

Checking a Customer's Card

  1. In Roster, click View Card on any customer to see their numbers in the real grid layout — handy for double-checking against their physical card.
  2. Below the grid, Progression lists every round they've played, what was drawn, and what they won.

Weekly Play Limit

  1. Customers are meant to play once a week. If someone already played in the last 7 days, you'll see a warning banner when you select them.
  2. It's a heads-up, not a hard stop — you can still start a round if there's a good reason to.

Backing Up Your Data

  1. In Roster, click Export Backup (JSON) regularly to save a full copy of everything outside the browser.
  2. Export Winners (CSV) gives a spreadsheet-friendly list of every win for record-keeping.
  3. Import Backup restores from a previous JSON export — this replaces all current data, so use it carefully.

Pro Tips: Promoting With RACBINGO

RACBINGO works best as a visible, talked-about reason to come back and to put more down. Example tiered structure:

  1. Standard weekly visit — a normal round.
  2. Start or renew an agreement with 2 weeks paid down — 2x the draws in their round.
  3. Pay a full month down — 3x the draws + 1 BONUS draw on top.

Treat these numbers as a starting example, not a fixed rule — set whatever tiers make sense for your store's current promotion, and post them somewhere customers can see.

Running bonus draws in the app

Since a round has no limit on balls, bonus draws are simple: just keep pulling balls for the customer, right through their standard amount and into their earned bonus balls, all in the same round. Click Submit Round / Next Customer only once you're completely done with all of it — everything gets saved together.

About the weekly alert

Since bonus balls happen inside the same round now, the "already played this week" warning only checks once, when you start the round — it won't interrupt you repeatedly while you draw their bonus balls.

  1. Celebrate wins loudly — the fireworks and popup are made for the moment. Let nearby customers see it happen.
  2. Post the current bonus-draw tiers near the register or play area so customers ask about them on their own.
  3. Run limited-time pushes (e.g. "Double Draw Weekend") to create urgency around paying more down.
  4. Hand out the printed card's back (rules + store info) as a takeaway reminder to come back next week.
  5. Check Roster → View Card before offering a promo to confirm what they've already played and won this week.
