Finding or Adding a Customer

  1. Go to Play Round.
  2. Type the customer's name in the search box.
  3. Click their name if they already exist, or click "+ Add ... as new customer" if they don't.

How the Card Works

  1. Each customer plays on one ongoing card — it isn't reset every week. They keep drawing a few new balls each visit, week after week, progressing toward Line, Four Corners, and Full BINGO on that same card, until they redeem a win or you close out their card.
  2. If a customer already has a card in progress, selecting them jumps straight into it — their full ball history and card marks are right there, picking up exactly where they left off.
  3. If they don't have one yet (brand new, or their last game was just closed out), you'll land on the card setup screen instead.

Setting Up a New Card

  1. Click Generate Random Card for an instant valid card, or type in all 24 numbers by hand from a physical card.
  2. Click Print Card (Front/Back) to hand them a physical copy — print the front, flip the card over, then print the back. This is the one card they'll keep using until they redeem or you close out their game.
  3. Click Start Game once the card shows "Card ready ✓".

Playing Each Weekly Visit

  1. Customers are meant to draw a handful of new balls each week — about 5 is the usual pace, though there's no hard limit built in. As each ball is called from the machine, type the number and click Draw (or press Enter).
  2. If the same number gets pulled twice for that customer — ever, not just this visit — the app rejects it and tells you to pull another ball.
  3. Made a mistake this visit? Click any drawn ball to remove just that one, or click Undo Last Draw for the most recent one. This works on any ball in their history, not just today's.
  4. Watch the Live Card Progress grid — matched numbers highlight green as you go, reflecting every ball ever drawn for that card, not just this visit.
  5. Click Done for Now / Next Customer once you're finished drawing for this visit — everything's already saved, so this just clears the screen for the next person. Their card stays exactly as it is until they come back.

Wins and Celebrations

  1. The app checks for a win after every ball — Line = $25, Four Corners = $75, Full BINGO = $100.
  2. Each tier only pays out once per card, no matter how many weeks it takes to get there. Once any single row, column, or diagonal completes, that's the one $25 Line prize — finishing more lines afterward doesn't add another. The next milestone up is Four Corners, then Full BINGO.
  3. A win triggers fireworks and a popup, then Continue Playing ▶ keeps things going with all progress intact. A card is worth one certificate, not one per milestone — it always reflects the running total earned so far, up to $200 ($25 + $75 + $100) if it reaches a full Blackout.

Redeeming or Closing Out a Game

  1. Right from the Play Round screen, click Redeem $[amount] & Start New Card (or Close Out & Start New Card if nothing's been won) whenever the customer is ready to cash in or start fresh. This logs the game as done, resets their card for next time, and takes you straight into card setup for their new one.
  2. A customer can redeem at any point — you don't need to wait for them to reach Blackout.
  3. Click Print Certificate anytime, before or after redeeming, to hand over (or reprint) their certificate — it always shows the card's current total, and reaching Full BINGO turns it into a grand $200 certificate automatically.

Fixing Mistakes (Any Time)

  1. For an in-progress card, click any drawn ball on the Play Round screen to remove it — that's the way to correct a mistake without closing out the whole game.
  2. For a closed game, go to History and click Edit Round to reassign it to the correct customer, add or remove drawn balls, or delete it entirely.
  3. Editing balls automatically rechecks whether it should show a win, so prizes stay accurate.
  4. Deleting a round cannot be undone — double-check before confirming.

Checking a Customer's Card

  1. In Roster, click View Card on any customer to see their numbers in the real grid layout — handy for double-checking against their physical card.
  2. Below the grid, Progression lists their current in-progress game (if any) plus every past closed game, what was drawn, and what they won.
  3. The Roster card itself shows an at-a-glance summary for anyone mid-game: which week they're on, total balls drawn, whether they've drawn this week yet, and a progress bar toward Blackout.

Weekly Pacing

  1. If someone already had balls drawn in the last 7 days, you'll see a note when you pull up their game. It's a heads-up, not a hard stop — you can still draw more if there's a good reason to.
  2. There's no limit built into the app on how many balls get drawn in one visit — the ~5-per-week pace is a house guideline for staff to follow, not something the software enforces.

Backing Up Your Data

  1. In Roster, click Export Backup (JSON) regularly to save a full copy of everything outside the browser — this is the only way to recover in-progress cards if something happens to this device.
  2. Export Winners (CSV) gives a spreadsheet-friendly list of every win for record-keeping, including customers still mid-game with an unredeemed running total.
  3. Import Backup restores from a previous JSON export — this replaces all current data, so use it carefully.
Tips & Scripts for Associates

Scripts You Can Use

Quick lines for common moments — adjust to your own voice, but these hit the key points.

Introducing the game to someone who hasn't played

"Hey [Name], have you played RACBINGO yet? It's free, it takes two minutes, and every week you're with us you get a few more shots at winning real RAC Cash on the same card — no purchase required. Want to get your card started right now?"

Announcing a win

"Hold on — that's a BINGO! 🎉 You just won $[amount] in RAC Cash. I'm printing your certificate right now, so hang onto it and we'll get you set up whenever you're ready to use it."

Pitching a bonus-draw promotion

"Since you're already playing, did you know if you put [X] down on an agreement today, you get [Y] bonus RACBINGO draws before you even leave? That's [Y]x the chances to walk out with RAC Cash."

Sending them off for the week

"You're all set for this week! Same card keeps building next time you're in — come back next [day] and we'll pull a few more balls for you."

Pro Tips: Promoting With RACBINGO

RACBINGO works best as a visible, talked-about reason to come back and to put more down. Example tiered structure:

  1. Standard weekly visit — the usual handful of draws.
  2. Start or renew an agreement with 2 weeks paid down — 2x the draws this visit.
  3. Pay a full month down — 3x the draws + 1 BONUS draw on top.

Treat these numbers as a starting example, not a fixed rule — set whatever tiers make sense for your store's current promotion, and post them somewhere customers can see.

Running bonus draws in the app

Bonus draws are simple: just keep pulling balls for the customer, right through their standard visit amount and into their earned bonus balls — there's no limit on how many go into one visit. Click Done for Now / Next Customer once you're completely done for the day.

  1. Celebrate wins loudly — the fireworks and popup are made for the moment. Let nearby customers see it happen.
  2. Post the current bonus-draw tiers near the register or play area so customers ask about them on their own.
  3. Run limited-time pushes (e.g. "Double Draw Weekend") to create urgency around paying more down.
  4. Hand out the printed card's back (rules + store info) as a takeaway reminder to come back next week.
  5. Check Roster → View Card before offering a promo to confirm where they stand and what they've already won.
