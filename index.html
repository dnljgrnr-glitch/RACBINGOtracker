Finding or Adding a Customer

  1. Go to Play Round.
  2. Type the customer's name in the search box.
  3. Click their name if they already exist, or click "+ Add ... as new customer" if they don't.

Setting Up Their Card

  1. Once a customer is selected, click Generate Random Card for an instant valid card, or type in all 24 numbers by hand from a physical card.
  2. Click Print Card (Front/Back) to hand them a physical copy — print the front, flip the card over, then print the back.

Starting and Playing a Round

  1. Once the card shows "Card ready ✓", click Start Round.
  2. As each ball is called from the machine, type the number and click Draw (or press Enter).
  3. If the same number gets pulled twice in one round, the app rejects it — just draw again. It doesn't count against the 5.
  4. Made a mistake? Click any drawn ball to remove just that one, or click Undo Last Draw for the most recent one.
  5. Watch the Live Card Progress grid — matched numbers highlight green as you go.

Wins and Celebrations

  1. The app checks for a win after every ball — Line = $25, Four Corners = $75, Full BINGO = $100.
  2. A win triggers fireworks and a popup. Click Print Certificate to hand the customer a redeemable certificate, then Notify Customer ✓ to close it.

Ending or Canceling a Round

  1. Click End Round / Next Customer when the customer is done playing — this saves their draws and any wins, and frees up the machine for the next person.
  2. If you started a round for the wrong customer by mistake, click Cancel Round (Wrong Customer) instead — nothing gets saved, and their weekly play status is untouched.

Redeeming a Reward

  1. Go to History.
  2. Find the win and click Confirm Redemption & New Card — this logs today's date and takes you straight to starting their next round.
  3. Reprint a certificate anytime with Print Certificate, even after it's been redeemed.

Fixing Mistakes (Any Time)

  1. In History, click Edit Round on any past round to reassign it to the correct customer, add or remove drawn balls, or delete the round entirely.
  2. Editing balls automatically rechecks whether the round should show a win, so prizes stay accurate.
  3. Deleting a round cannot be undone — double-check before confirming.

Checking a Customer's Card

  1. In Roster, click View Card on any customer to see their numbers in the real grid layout — handy for double-checking against their physical card.
  2. Below the grid, Progression lists every round they've played, what was drawn, and what they won.

Weekly Play Limit

  1. Customers are meant to play once a week. If someone already played in the last 7 days, you'll see a warning banner when you select them.
  2. It's a heads-up, not a hard stop — you can still start a round if there's a good reason to.

Backing Up Your Data

  1. In Roster, click Export Backup (JSON) regularly to save a full copy of everything outside the browser.
  2. Export Winners (CSV) gives a spreadsheet-friendly list of every win for record-keeping.
  3. Import Backup restores from a previous JSON export — this replaces all current data, so use it carefully.
